# Brewery photos

Optional. Save a photo as `img/<breweryid>.jpg`, for example `img/berlinerberg.jpg`. The Breweries page shows it as a
circle, cropped from the centre, so a square or slightly wide photo with the subject in the middle works best.

- Resize to about 400 px on the long side before committing (keeps the page fast on a phone).
- A brewery without a photo shows its initials instead.
- The ids are the keys in `data/breweries.json`.
- Use only your own photos, or ones you have permission to use. Do not copy photos from Google Maps or other sites.
